A NOTE ON CREATION

Many people say
coding with AI is simple.

That you just ask,
and the machine creates.

But what they don’t see
is the creativity behind the idea,
the patience behind every fix,
and the persistence behind every failure.

AI doesn’t imagine timelines.
AI doesn’t feel tension.
AI doesn’t understand consequence.

It only writes what it is told.

You decide the vision.
You decide the emotion.
You decide when something feels right.

AI is the instrument.
You are the composer.

TIME IS NOT A TOOL.
IT IS A CONSEQUENCE.
Use it wisely.
